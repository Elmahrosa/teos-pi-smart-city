## 🪙 Pioneer Funding Model

### Treasury Target
- **Pioneers:** 10,000+
- **Minimum Stake:** 1,000 Pi per pioneer
- **Initial Treasury Goal:** 10,000,000 Pi
- **Expansion Goal:** Scalable based on land acquisition strategy

### Signing Fee
- **5 Pi per pioneer** to join governance
- **Total Signing Revenue:** 50,000+ Pi (for governance operations)

### Civic Share Allocation
- **1,000 Pi = 1 civic share** (Alexandria zone)
- Shares are **non-transferable** outside Pi Network
- Shares remain valid under **Dynamic Area Clause**

### Treasury Usage
- Land acquisition strategy (partnerships, phased approach)
- Construction phasing
- Operational costs (schools, hospitals, security)
- Transparent logging in public ledger
